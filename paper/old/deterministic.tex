\section{Deterministic algorithm}


Random approximate perfect hash function.


Perfect hash function maker.
Default.
\begin{equation}
\ph_{\Set{X}} \colon \PS{\Set{U}} \times \RealSet \mapsto \IntSet_{\geq 0}\,.
\end{equation}


This is sort of what we already have.
\begin{equation}
\ph_{\Set{X}} \colon \Set{U} \OMap \IntSet_{\geq 0}\,.
\end{equation}


With the cipher monad, we kind of get this
\begin{equation}
\ph \colon \PS{\OT{\Set{U}}} \times \RealSet \OMap \IntSet_{\geq 0}\,.
\end{equation}

Returning a cipher integer, $\OT{\IntSet_{\geq 0}}$, may not have that much of a point, or maybe in some cases it does.
